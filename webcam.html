Complete Online Webcam Tester

Use our free camera testing utility to ensure you are ready for your next video call, stream, or online exam. By checking your webcam beforehand, you can adjust your lighting, fix your framing, and verify that your hardware is transmitting a clean signal.

How to Test Your Camera Online

Click the "Request Camera Access" button above. Your browser will prompt you for permission to use your camera. Once approved, your live video feed will appear. We apply a mirror effect to the feed, making it behave exactly like a physical mirror for easier framing.

100% Private and Secure

Privacy is our top priority. This webcam tester runs entirely within your local browser. No video data, streams, or snapshots are ever recorded, saved, or uploaded to our servers. What happens on your screen stays on your device.
